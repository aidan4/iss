# Third-party acknowledgements

The focus-transition implementation in `focus_switch.c` adapts yabai's
SIP-compatible focus-notification and synthetic Space-switching approach.
The synthetic IOHID layout in `focus_event.h` follows the protocol documented
by noswoosh for macOS 27. ISS keeps its own swipe reducer and timed slides.

- yabai: https://github.com/asmvik/yabai
- noswoosh: https://github.com/mmathys/noswoosh
